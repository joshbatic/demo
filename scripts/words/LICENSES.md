# Word library: sources and licences

*[demo]'s rhymes, syllables, synonyms, related words, opposites and definitions are built by `build.py` from
these open sources. Their notices are reproduced below, as their licences ask.

- **CMU Pronouncing Dictionary** (Carnegie Mellon University): pronunciations, used for rhymes and syllable counts. BSD-style licence, below.
- **WordNet 3.0** (Princeton University): synonyms, related words, opposites, definitions and phrases. WordNet licence, below.
- **wordfreq** (Robyn Speer): word frequencies, used only to order words from common to rare. The code is Apache-2.0;
  its frequency data is CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The 0–9 commonness numbers
  in the rhyme dictionary are derived from it and are shared under the same licence.
