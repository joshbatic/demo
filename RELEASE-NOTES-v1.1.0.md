# *[demo] 1.1.0

Released on the web and Mac on 2 Nov 2026. The iPhone app gets it as its next App Store update, after 1.0 is approved.

## What's new
- **Albums.** Gather songs into an album, EP or single, with artwork, a tracklist you can reorder, and a page laid out like Apple Music on your artwork's colours. Read the whole album's lyrics in one go. One album on Free, as many as you like with Pro.
- **Album share links.** Share an album as a link anyone can open; each song opens its own page, with a way back to the album.
- **Album artwork on its songs.** Songs in an album show the album's cover, in the app and on share pages.
- **Widgets (iPhone).** Record Idea and Continue Writing, in glass, for the Home Screen.
- **Export.** The song list's footer link is now just "Export".
- **Sign-in.** The sign-in screen says "Every song starts as a demo."; web sign-in returns to the right page.
