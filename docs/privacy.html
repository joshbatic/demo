Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026
*[demo] is in beta, available through Apple's TestFlight. This policy covers the iPhone and Mac apps, the website hellodemo.app, and share pages at listen.hellodemo.app.

Who we are

*[demo] is made by an independent developer based in South Australia, Australia ("we", "us"). We decide how your information is used and are responsible for it. You can reach us at joshuadbatic@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide it), your name if you share it, and an account ID.
  • Your profile. Your username, display name, and, if you add them, a photo and social links. People you write with can see these.
  • Your songs and ideas. Lyrics, titles, chords, notes, versions, artwork, the song brief and everything else you write in the app.
  • Audio and files. Voice memos, demos, mixes, stems and other files you add. WAV, AIFF and FLAC files are converted to MP3 on your device before they upload.
  • Muse memory. What you did with each AI suggestion (kept, rewrote, deleted or skipped, with the text) and the description of your writing style, so suggestions sound like you.
  • Usage counts. How many AI and lyric-sync requests your account makes, and when. We don't keep what was sent.
  • Error reports. When something goes wrong: the error message, the app's build number, your platform and which screen you were on. Reports never include your lyrics, and song and file names are removed.

The app uses the microphone only while you're recording, and only after you've allowed it. It uses the camera or photo library only when you choose a picture. We don't collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To run the app: saving and syncing your songs, playing your audio, sharing and collaboration. We need this to provide the service you signed up for.
  • To answer the AI features you use, and to make their suggestions fit your writing.
  • To keep the service fair, affordable and secure (usage limits and abuse prevention), and to find and fix bugs. These are our legitimate interests, and they use as little information as possible.
  • To reply when you contact us.

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or rent your information. We don't read your songs, except where a feature you use sends text to an AI provider to answer it, or you ask us to look at something for support.

AI features

Lines, Rhymes, Muse, Rate, Writing Voice and lyric sync work by sending the relevant text to an AI provider: OpenAI, or Anthropic (Claude) in the web version. Lyric sync sends a small, speech-quality copy of the audio with the lyrics as a hint. To make suggestions sound like you, the muse may also send a few of your closest lines from other songs, the song brief, and a few suggestions you kept or turned down.

The provider uses this only to answer that request. These providers don't train their models on data sent through their APIs by default, and we don't train any models on your work. If you'd rather nothing you write leaves your device, don't use the AI features. Rhyme lookups send the single word you're rhyming to Datamuse, a free word-finding service, without your account details.

Share links and collaboration

When you make a share link, a copy of that song is stored so that anyone with the link can open it in a browser without an account. The copy includes the title, artist, lyrics, line timings, colours, artwork and, if you choose, the audio. Links are random and can't be guessed or listed, but anyone you send one to can pass it on. Stop Sharing deletes the copy and its files.

If you invite a collaborator, they can see the song and your profile, and you can see theirs. Removing them stops further syncing, but it can't take back what they've already seen.

Who we share it with

We use these services to run *[demo]. They handle your information on our behalf, under their own privacy and security commitments.

  • Supabase (on Amazon Web Services): sign-in, database and file storage, in Seoul, South Korea.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic: AI features, as described above.
  • Datamuse: rhyme lookups.
  • Vercel: serves share pages.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • jsDelivr: delivers part of the app's code when it starts, so it sees your IP address.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, and TestFlight for the beta.

We'll also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Where it's stored

Your account, songs and files are stored in South Korea (Seoul), on Supabase. The other services are based mainly in the United States, so your information may also be processed there and in other countries outside Australia. We choose providers with strong security and privacy terms. Where the law requires it, such as for people in the EU and UK, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. Deleting a song, file or share link deletes it straight away. Deleting your account (Account › Delete Account) deletes everything stored for it, including usage counts and error reports. Copies may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for a short time, usually no more than a few weeks, until they're overwritten. Songs saved on a device stay there until you delete the app.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Your files are kept in private storage that only your account can open, and the database only lets each account read its own data and songs shared with it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information (you can also use Export All Songs in Account);
  • correct it (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete it (Account › Delete Account does this immediately);
  • stop or restrict a particular use, or object to it.

Email us and we'll respond within 30 days, free of charge. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), or to the data protection authority where you live, such as your EU supervisory authority or the UK ICO.

If you're in California: we don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, and we don't use sensitive information to infer things about you.

Children

You must be at least 13 to use *[demo]. If you're under 18, or under the age of digital consent where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and we'll always update the date at the top. If we ever want to use your information in a new way that needs your consent, we'll ask first.
