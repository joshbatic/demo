Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026
*[demo] is currently in private beta, distributed through TestFlight. This policy describes how the app handles your information today and may change as the app develops — see "Changes to this policy" below.

What this app is

*[demo] is a songwriting app for iPhone and Mac, with tools like rhyme suggestions, line suggestions, a rhyme map, syllable/melody checks, versions, audio versions synced to your lyrics, share links and optional collaboration with other people.

What's stored, and where

You sign in to *[demo] with Google or Apple. Your songs — lyrics, titles, chords, notes and the rest — are saved on your device and synced to your account, so they're backed up and available on your other devices.

Your account also has a profile (a username, display name and optionally a photo and social links) that people you write with can see.

Audio and files

If you add audio (voice memos, demos, mixes, masters), stems or other files to a song while signed in, they're stored in your account's private cloud storage so they sync to your other devices. Only you can read them; the app plays them through short-lived private links. WAV, AIFF and FLAC files are converted to MP3 on your device before they upload. Deleting a file or the song removes the stored copy.

Voice memos you record in Ideas are stored the same way. The app only uses the microphone while you're recording (a voice memo, or a melody you hum), and only after you've allowed it.

Automatic lyric sync sends a small, speech-quality copy of the current version (mono, low sample rate), plus the song's lyrics as a hint, to OpenAI's speech-to-text service to work out when each line is sung. As with the other AI features, it's used only to answer that request.

Share links

When you create a share link, a copy of that song (title, artist, lyrics, line timings, colours, artwork and, if you choose, the current audio version) is stored so that anyone with the link can open it in a browser without an account. Links use a random address that can't be guessed or listed, but anyone you send it to can pass it on. The copy changes only when you tap Update, and Stop Sharing deletes it and its files so the link stops working. Messaging apps that show link previews will fetch the song's title and preview picture when a link is sent.

AI features

Tools like Lines, Rhymes, Muse and Writing Voice work by sending the relevant text to an AI service (OpenAI's API, or, on the web version, Claude) to generate suggestions. That text is used to generate the response to that one request. We don't use it to train our own models, and as of this policy's last update OpenAI's API terms exclude API data from being used to train their models by default — but you should assume any text you send through these features leaves your device.

To make suggestions sound like you, the muse also sends a handful of your own lines from other songs that are closest to what you're writing, the song brief if you filled one in, and a few suggestions you previously kept, rewrote or threw out. Every so often it sends a sample of your songs to write a short description of your style, which you can read, edit or relearn in Writing voice. When you're signed in, what happened to each suggestion (kept, rewritten, deleted or skipped, with the text) and that style description are stored in your account so they follow you between devices. Only you can see them, and they're deleted with your account.

If you'd rather nothing you write is sent anywhere, you can use the app without touching the AI-powered tools; everything else works entirely offline.

Collaboration

If you invite someone to collaborate on a song, that song's content becomes visible to them, and your username/display name becomes visible to them as the owner (and vice versa). Removing a collaborator or turning sharing off stops further syncing, but doesn't retroactively erase what they already received.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or renting your data to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No reading your lyrics except where a feature you used sends them to an AI provider to generate that feature's response.

Usage limits and error reports

To keep AI features fair and affordable, the app counts how many AI and lyric-sync requests each account makes per day (just the count and time, not what was sent). When something goes wrong in the app, it sends a short error report (the error message, the app's build number, your platform and which screen you were on) so we can fix it. Reports never include your lyrics, and song or file names are removed from them. Both are deleted with your account.

Your choices

  • Delete any song, audio file or attachment at any time from within the app.
  • Stop sharing any share link from the song's Share sheet.
  • Delete your account at any time in Account › Delete Account. This deletes your songs, ideas, recordings, share links, profile and everything else stored for your account, on every device. It can't be undone.

Children

*[demo] isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

As the app moves from beta toward a public release, this policy will be updated to reflect it — including if payment, analytics or additional data uses are introduced. We'll update the date at the top when that happens.
