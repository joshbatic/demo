*[demo] for Mac

The same songs, synced with your iPhone. Sign in with the same Apple account you use there.

Download for Mac

macOS 11 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · about 200 MB

Installing

  1. Open demo-mac.dmgIt's in your Downloads folder.
  2. Drag *[demo] into ApplicationsThen open it from Applications or Launchpad.
  3. Approve it the first timemacOS may stop it from opening the first time. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Sign inWith Apple, the same account as on your iPhone. Your songs appear straight away.
