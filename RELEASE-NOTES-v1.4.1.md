# *[demo] 1.4.1

The settled version of 1.4: everything in 1.4.0, plus the iPhone extras and fixes below. On iPhone this is the App Store update after 1.3.0 (1.4.0 was never submitted there), so its "What's New" covers both.

## What's new
- **Rhyme of the Day widget (iPhone).** A new word every morning with its rhymes, in small, medium and Lock Screen sizes. Tap it to start a song.
- **New widgets (iPhone).** Record Idea, Continue Writing and Rhyme of the Day follow Apple's own layout: a small label on top, your words in system type, round buttons, and glass that looks right on a Clear or Tinted Home Screen.
- **Liquid Glass app icon (iPhone).** On a Clear or Tinted Home Screen the icon becomes a glass asterisk on a frosted disc.

## Fixes
- Co-writer changes show their photo once, beside the last thing they changed, not on every changed line.
- iPhone: the rhyme map shows as soon as it's switched on (before, it only appeared after syllable counts were toggled).
- Lines (Pro) no longer fails with "Could not get suggestions" when the AI runs long (server fix, already live).

## App Store "What's New" (iPhone)
Bring your songs in: share a note from Notes or a recording from Voice Memos straight into *[demo], or import lyric files, PDFs and pasted songs.
See what a co-writer changed since you last opened a shared song, and live co-writing that stays steady while you both type.
A simpler Ideas list where voice memos play right in the list.
Smarter rhymes that follow the stressed sound.
New widgets, including Rhyme of the Day, and a Liquid Glass icon for Clear and Tinted Home Screens.
Plus fixes: the rhyme map shows straight away, Lines is more reliable, and signing in again keeps your name.
