# *[demo] 1.2.0

Released on the web and Mac on 3 Nov 2026. The iPhone app gets it as its next App Store update, after 1.0 is approved.

## What's new
- **Find Release.** Look a song up in Apple Music from Details › Release and fill in its ISRC, UPC, release date, label, genre and ISWC in one go.
- **Key and tempo from your audio.** An empty Key and BPM fill themselves in from the song's audio when you add a version (change them in Details if they're off).
- **Perform at the song's tempo.** With a tempo set, Perform mode scrolls the words at the song's pace; the speed buttons still nudge it.
- **How *[demo] works.** The guide song is now an illustrated walkthrough with common questions.
- **New Song, Folder or Album.** Press and hold the new button in the song list for all three; a tap still starts a song.
- **Account and links.** Glass tiles with clearer icons, and the real Spotify, Apple Music, Instagram and TikTok marks on your links.
- **Widgets (iPhone).** Softer tinted-glass colours.

## Fixes and polish
- Fewer pop-up messages, and the ones left go away sooner.
- Inviting a co-writer feels instant: results narrow as you type and Invite confirms straight away.
- Deleting a song from the list no longer flashes it back while you confirm, and swipe-to-delete stays red in dark mode.
