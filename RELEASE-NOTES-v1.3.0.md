# *[demo] 1.3.0

Released on the web and Mac on 18 Nov 2026, and submitted to the App Store as the iPhone app's first update (it brings 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 to iPhone together).

## What's new
- **Ideas, redesigned.** Ideas read like a list in Notes: one rounded list per day, each idea with a small coloured label (Line, Title, Concept, Memo) and its words in full. A mic button beside search records a memo straight away, and an idea can be a Title or a Concept as well as a Line.
- **Co-write in every song's ••• menu.** It goes straight to Invite a Co-writer (sharing the song first if needed). Credits has its own icon.
- **A new Pro screen.** Five moving pages show what Pro adds; the spinning records sit neatly inside the page.
- **A much bigger word library, offline.** 68,669 rhyming words and 146,000 words and phrases with synonyms, related words, opposites, definitions and rhyming phrases, built in.
- **Smarter rhyme order.** Rhymes with the same number of syllables come first, everyday words next, and words tied to your song can jump ahead.
- **Co-writing that holds up offline.** Changes made apart are merged instead of the newest one winning, and demos stay playable when a co-writer leaves.
- **Share links** play the song's own audio and last 30 days; the share sheet says when a link stops working.
- **Albums without artwork** show a glass *[demo] record on the album's colour.

## Fixes and polish
- iPhone editor: tapping or selecting no longer scrolls the page.
- "Time to update" opens the App Store page.
- Security and reliability: tighter limits on AI use, safer sign-in links and sign-ups, checked web scripts, and a newer, hardened Mac app.
