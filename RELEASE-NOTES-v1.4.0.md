# *[demo] 1.4.0

Released on the web and Mac on 20 Nov 2026, and the next App Store update for iPhone.

## What's new
- **Bring your songs in.** A new screen in Account › Import Songs (and the song list's Import Songs) for every way in: lyric files (text, and on iPhone PDF and Rich Text, lots at once), pasting a song, and on iPhone, Notes and Voice Memos.
- **Save to *[demo] from Notes and Voice Memos (iPhone).** Share a note to make it a new song, add it to the end of a song, or keep it as an idea; share a recording and it lands in Ideas, ready to start a song. Headings like "Chorus" or "Verse 2:" on their own line become sections.
- **See what a co-writer changed.** Opening a shared song shows what changed since you last had it open on that device: the changed words are lightly highlighted, with a cursor and the co-writer's photo, and "Sam changed 3 lines · 1 hr ago" under the title takes you to the first change. They go when you start typing, after a minute and a half, or when you leave the song.
- **Ideas, simpler.** No more coloured labels: each idea is its words with a grey line for the time (and a small symbol for a Title or Concept). Voice memos have a play button and play right in the list, with a waveform to drag through.
- **Smarter rhymes.** The rhyme bar only suggests words that rhyme from the stressed sound ("getting" → setting, forgetting, wedding, not sting or thing), adds rhymes the online list missed, and puts words in the same form first.

## Fixes and polish
- Live co-writing feels steadier: a co-writer typing where your cursor is no longer moves your cursor or your rhyme bar, and their edits no longer make the page jump.
- Changing your name no longer marks every song as just edited, and signing in again keeps your name and username without asking.
- Syncing renews an expired sign-in by itself instead of showing "JWT expired".
- Share pages' "Written in *[demo]" goes to the App Store.
- iPhone: a gentle request for an App Store rating after you've written a few songs.
